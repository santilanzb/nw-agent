## 1. The ruling and why

### 1.1 What happened across the two workflows

- **Fan-out synthesis (2026-07-08)** declared **C2** (Pydantic AI v2 + DBOS "gutty-core") the provisional winner by 0.07 overall, explicitly conditional on a Stage-0 spike, with C1 as the pre-committed no-relitigation fallback. All three fan-out judges agreed on the hinge: *"C2's margin exists if and only if the Stage-0 DBOSAgent spike holds."*
- **Tournament duels (2026-07-10):** C2 defeated C3 (margin 5), C4 (margin 6), and C5 (margin 6) — but **lost the head-to-head final to C1, 2 referees to 1**. The upset was declared on verified evidence, not scoreboard anchoring (see §2).
- **Red-team (6 lanes, all vs the survivor base C1 + synthesis grafts):** every lane returned **`graft_required`; zero lanes returned `base_breaking`** (see §3).

### 1.2 Why the duel upset stands under final review

The fan-out verdict's own stated hinge failed verification, independently, three times:

1. **The DBOSAgent deferred-tool/HITL pause-resume mechanism — the exact primitive C2 routed its autonomy ladder (F6), tee-ups (F9), and discount escalation through — is undocumented on the official Pydantic AI DBOS integration page** (https://pydantic.dev/docs/ai/integrations/durable_execution/dbos/, fetched 2026-07-10 by refs 1, 3, and the facts lane). Worse, the facts lane confirmed the documented semantics contradict C2-as-written: a deferred tool **ends** the agent run with `DeferredToolRequests`; resumption is a **new run** fed persisted history + `DeferredToolResults` (https://ai.pydantic.dev/deferred-tools/). There is no documented "paused workflow resumed in place." C2 §3's flagship approvals text is wrong as written, and the synthesis spike rubric item (b) tested a primitive that does not exist in the API shape.
2. **DBOS version-pinned recovery is verified real and operationally heavy for this team** (https://docs.dbos.dev/production/self-hosting/workflow-recovery): workflows only recover onto processes running the app version that started them. C2's highest-value subsystem — per-lead, weeks-long durable-sleep cadence enrollments — becomes deploy-fragile on a single-droplet, weekly-deploy, 1–3-person operation. `DBOS.patch()` / `application_version` pinning exist (ref 2's fair counterpoint) but constitute a standing discipline tax. The honest fix inside C2 is tables+ticks — **C1's cadence design**. When the winner's flagship subsystem, corrected, becomes the loser's design, the architecture signal is clear.
3. **The precision margin that broke the fan-out tie (8.33 vs 7.67) does not survive side-by-side reading:** five of C2's seven §6 guards are the same hand-written code as C1 §5 with the same admitted at-least-once residual window. The honest differentials — checkpointed side-effect turns, mechanical key derivation, one HITL primitive, import-typed packages — are all **graftable onto C1 at module blast-radius** (advocate grafts G-a..G-f; formalized in §4 below). Additionally, the facts lane proved DBOSAgent **pickles raw prompts and model responses into DBOS checkpoint blobs by documented design**, making synthesis graft G4's structural no-raw-text rule *impossible* for C2's conversation reactor — eroding the compliance-precision tiebreak that made C2 the fan-out winner in the first place.

Structural tiebreaker, endorsed by refs 1 and 3: **option-value asymmetry.** C1 + Pydantic AI à la carte can adopt DBOSAgent/deferred-tools later, once that surface is documented and stability-covered (a standing Stage-5 decision point). C2 cannot cheaply un-adopt a substrate under 100% of execution after packages exist as framework-typed objects. The distinguishing risks (3-month major-version churn window — verified: https://pydantic.dev/docs/ai/project/version-policy/; the 20th deploy over a live cadence book; vendor cliff) materialize months in, after the 2-week spike would have passed. A gate that almost certainly passes is a ceremony, not a gate.

Fairness to C2, recorded: its uniform-durability concept remains "the strongest single architectural idea in the study" (operator-pragmatist judge); its three compounding advantages are honestly conceded by C1's own advocate; ref 2's dissent (spike-gated bounded bet; grafts amount to hand-rebuilding C2 inside C1, evidence C2 is the attractor) is the strongest argument for C2 and is honored **by grafting the attractor's virtues, not by adopting the substrate**. If NutriWhite hires a second backend engineer or the deferred-tool surface becomes documented and stable, the balance genuinely re-opens — hence the standing Stage-5 re-decision point (graft 7).

### 1.3 Why no red-team lane breaks the base

Two lanes carry `fatal`-severity findings; neither breaks C1:

- **PHI lane fatals (OpenAI embedding leak; Batch BAA laundering)** are orthogonal to the orchestration choice: *every* candidate reuses the same rag-api embedding classifier and the same Batch-economics idea, so they discriminate between no candidates. Fixes are vendor contracts + a component swap + process gates (grafts 9–10), not architecture.
- **Ops lane fatal (DBOS deploy-versioning vs long-sleeping workflows)** binds C2's cadence shape; C1's cadence is already relational rows + scheduled ticks. The graft codifies it as design law and adds the deploy runbook for the remaining short sagas (graft 1).
- **Cost lane fatal (F4 silently repriced)** corrects the published number and adds a governor + user sign-off; the architecture is unchanged (graft 17).
- **Chaos lane** found real seam holes (Zoho in-doubt window, turn-durability contradiction, approval TOCTOU) — all closable with mechanisms C1 already owns (WAL, outbox, ladder), now specified precisely (grafts 2–6).
- **Eighty lane** reshapes the autonomy ladder and human surface (grafts 15, 18–19) without touching the spine.

**VERDICT: `confirmed_with_grafts`.** Final base = **C1 Evolve-Current v3**, with the 20 mandatory grafts of §4, the Stage-0 rubric of §5, and the honest re-baselines of §6.
